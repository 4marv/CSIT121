Why is it important?

High Resolution Time Lvel 2 is important as it gives developers very accurate timing data. This kind of accuracy helps improve overall website performance and UX. Without it, measuring small performance differences would be much more harder. Even small tiny delays in processing can add up eventually, and affect the interactions of a website.

It also helps identify any slow running parts of a website or application. Developers can see exactly which functions are taking too long to run. This helps developers optimize code and make websites faster overall.

Many modern web apps, games, and animations rely on precise timing. Even small delays can affect how smooth something feels. This standard helps reduce those issues by giving better measurement tools. For example, a game might feel pretty laggy, or an animation might stutter a little if the timing is not measure accurately.
